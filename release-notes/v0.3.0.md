The two agents' reviews of a commit are posted as one, with duplicates merged and every item credited to the reviewer that found it. Reviewers may end the review of a trivial PR early.

- **One post per commit:**
  - A finished review waits for the other agent's review of the same commit: for as long as that one runs, and for up to `combine_wait` (30 minutes) while it waits its turn.
  - The agent that finishes last then runs a short duplicate check, with the same locked-down flags as its review. An issue both reviewers reported appears once, credited to both, with a note when they rated it differently.
  - Every finding, nit, test gap, question and suggested change in a combined post says who found it. Follow-ups are combined too, with each reviewer's verdict on every previous finding.
  - A review is posted on its own when the other one failed or is still queued after the wait, and with `combine_wait = "0s"`.
  - If the duplicate check fails, times out or hits a usage limit, both reviews are posted in full, with a note.
  - Post retries and crash recovery cover the combined post as a whole, so it is posted once.
  - `review` without `--post` also writes the combined review, as it would be posted, to the last job's `out/round.md`.
- **Trivial PRs:** a reviewer may judge a PR trivial (it only changes strings, comments or docs, bumps a version or renames something) and end the review early, after checking the change itself. The review says so.
- **Review data:**
  - Each reviewer's own review is still recorded.
  - An item merged into another reviewer's has `duplicate_of` set. Follow-ups don't ask about it again, and credit its reviewer on the one they do ask about.
  - Jobs record whether the reviewer judged the PR trivial (`trivial`) and which job's post carried their review (`posted_with`). `findings --json` includes both, and `findings` marks duplicates.

The database gains one migration. Upgrade while no `local-autoreviewer review` command is running; the worker restarts itself on the new version once it is idle. Before going back to 0.2.0, let reviews in the new `waiting` status post first: 0.2.0 doesn't know that status and would leave them unposted.
