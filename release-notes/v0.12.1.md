A fix that removes lines no longer raises a finding's severity.

- **Severity:** until now, a finding whose fix removed lines outside the tests moved up a level, so a rare race with a three-line fix could be posted as Major ("raised from minor: removes 3 lines"). Authors found that hard to read and backwards: the size of a fix says nothing about how bad the problem is. The severity now stays the reviewer's, and the finding's rating says what the fix removes instead ("the fix removes 3 lines"). A fix the reviewer rates less readable still moves the finding down two levels.
