Follow-ups stop asking about findings you said you won't fix.

- **`declined` status:** a finding you decided not to fix came back as "not fixed" in every follow-up until the PR merged. On upscale-relay#58 the deferred PQ blending did so in four rounds, and on #60 the feedback asked for exactly this change. A follow-up now reads what you wrote on the PR since its first review: replies in the review's threads (with the heading of the suggestion each answers), comments, and reviews. A finding you said you won't fix gets the status `declined`, with where you said it and your reason, and isn't asked about again. The reviewer can say if it thinks your reason doesn't hold. A finding you didn't mention that is still there stays `not_fixed`.
- **Numbered references and whole-PR reviews:** a comment that isn't a thread reply carries the numbered findings of the review it answers, so "won't fix 2" can be matched to its finding. A declined finding goes into `already-raised.json`, with the follow-up's account of your reason, so a follow-up that reviews the whole PR doesn't report it again.
- **Whose comments:** only the PR author's comments are read, which for the service means yours. Other people's comments, and the service's own reviews and suggestions (recognised by their marker and the account that posts them), are left out. The latest 60 are kept, each up to 3,000 characters. If GitHub can't list them, the follow-up goes on without them.
- **Review data:** `resolutions.status` can now be `declined`.
